      Frequently asked questions

      When does the Fall 2026 semester start?

      Classes begin on September 1, 2026. Orientation week runs from August 24 to August 28.

      Can I register for more than one course?

      Yes. Submit the registration form once for each course you want to take.

      Are online study options available?

      Most courses offer an online mode with live-streamed lectures and recorded sessions. Choose "Online" when registering.

      How do I contact the admissions office?

      Email admissions@northbridge.edu or call +1 (555) 012-3400, Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:00.
